Ice Strike Invoker Monk Build Guide

Early AccessThe Third Edict - 0.3

Last Updated:October 8, 2025|Changelog|FAQ

This Ice Strike Build Guide for the Monk class teaches you how to build your character with step-by-step explanations.

Whilst melee struggles in the early Acts, this build becomes a monster as soon as you gain access to Charged Staff in Act 3. Rapid hits from Ice Strike and Charged Staff on your Tempest Bell make the campaign bosses a complete joke.

During mapping, combine Ice Strike with Shattering Palm to deal massive area damage.

In the late game this build is an absolute tank reaching over 15000 Energy Shield when using Meditate whilst still being able to explode screens of enemies. Freeze also serves as a great defensive tool to back up your massive Energy Shield pool.

Note: You do not need resistances on any specific item, just get the required total resistances amongst all gear combined.

Leveling Guide

You beat the campaign and want know how to further upgrade this build? Visit the Endgame Guide!

Ice Strike combined with Tempest Bell is your main source of damage during the campaign.

During Act 1, before reaching Geonor, use Glacial Cascade. It's very important to complete specific encounters during Act 1 to have the required Skill Gems.

  • Miller Reward - Killing Palm
  • Hidden Cache (Clearfell) - Frost Bomb
  • Brambleghast (Grelwood) - Frozen Locus
  • Red Vale Boss - Glacial Cascade
  • Witches Hut (Grelwood) - Uncut Support Gem
  • Rotten Druid (Grim Tangle) - Uncut Support Gem

To make the Geonor fight much easier, It's important to find 2 additional Uncut Support Gems before the end of Act 1. They can be found from the following sources:

  • Rare Monster Event (Osinia's Mausoleum)
  • Rare Monster Event (Hunting Grounds)
  • Rare Monster Even (Ogham Farmlands)
  • Hanging Corpse (Manor Ramparts)

Use Glacial Cascade until you reach the final boss of Act 1, Count Geonor. At this point, swap to the Ice Strike embed, and don't look back.

  • Ice Strike and Herald of Ice easily deal with packs of Normal and Magic enemies.
  • Use Tempest Bell against Rare Monsters, Bosses and in any situation where you are getting overwhelmed by monsters.
  • Charged Staff adds a large damage bonus to your attacks as well as a shockwave to increase AoE.
    • In order to maintain this buff, you need to consume Power Charges. These are generated with the combination of Combat Frenzy and Resonance.
  • Shattering Palm massively increases your clear. Use this on large packs such as abyss'.

There are no substantial skill changes for the campaign outside of Support gem additions past this point, follow the gem setups on the right to continue.

  • Hand of Chayula and it's Supports enable Charge Regulation which grants a massive amount of Skill Speed, Crit Chance and Defence.
  • The Endgame setup requires 220 spirit, this requires a very good Body Armour due to Lead me through Grace….
    • If you don't have enough spirit, remove Elemental Conflux and replace it with Convalescence for additional Energy Shield Recovery.
  • Rakiata's Flow is a massive damage increase to Elemental Damage builds, but also makes Sunder my Enemies… obselete. Make sure to swap it out once purchasing or finding a Rakiata's Flow.

Pre Ice Strike

  • Use Frost Bomb to have full uptime of the debuff.
  • Frozen Locus to build distance from the boss.
  • Glacial Cascade so that the tip of the attack hits the boss.

Bosses

  • Always try to carry Power Charges or a stacked Charged Staff into a Boss encounter.
    • Freezing Tempest Bell also counts for Combat Frenzy to gain Power Charges against bosses..
  • Generate 4 combo points with Ice Strike.
  • Place your Tempest Bell directly underneath the boss.
  • Generate 4 more combo points with Ice Strike
  • Place your 2nd Tempest Bell.
  • Ice Strike until both the Tempest Bell have no hits left.
  • By now the boss will almost always be dead, if not simply restart the rotation.
  • Once you unlock Shattering Palm it vastly increases your clear and speed.
  • Engage large packs of enemies with Shattering Palm and then explode them with your Ice Strike.
  • Hand of Chayula frequently to build power charges and frenzy charges via Sniper's Mark and Ailith's Chimes.

Stat Priorities

Early Access

Offensive Scaling

  • Attack Speed is king in the Endgame as it increases the speed at which you move with Tempest Flurry.
  • You increase your DPS from your weapon by looking for the following stats, in order;
    • Attack Speed
    • +% Increased Elemental Damage with Attacks
    • Critical Hit Chance
    • % increased Physical Damage
    • Adds # to # Physical Damage
    • Adds # to # Cold/Fire/Lightning
    • The difference between Physical and Elemental damage is negligible, look for the highest EDPS or PDPS weapon after getting the first few mods.
  • Attack Skill Levels are amazing for the build and can be gotten exclusively with the Perfect Essence of Battle. This scales ALL your attack skills, including Charged Staff and Wind Dancer.

Defensive Scaling

  • During the Campaign and early maps, focus on getting more Life.
  • Sufficient +#% to Fire, Cold, Lightning and Chaos Resistance to cap your Resistances.
  • Maximum Energy Shield.
  • Evasion Rating.
  • Having a source of Mana Leech is essential in the Endgame, combined with Walker of the Wilds it makes up the entirety of your mana sustain.
  • Try to get anywhere between 50 and 100 rarity for optimal loot.

Gearing

Early Access
Ice Strike Invoker
Campaign
Maps
Endgame
  • During the campaign, your main focus is finding a powerful weapon. Always look to get the highest DPS Quarterstaff base with additional good modifiers.
    • Attack Speed helps with hitting fast mobs early on.
  • Check vendors for gear with any Dex or Dex/Int bases with Life or Resistances and use your crafting materials on them.
  • Flat Damage on Rings and Gloves provide a lot of additional damage.
  • Charged Staff requires Power Charges to be cast for the additional damage and shockwaves. These are achieved by speccing into the Resonance Keystone and using Combat Frenzy.
  • Shattering Palm into packs off enemies and explode them with your Ice Strike for clear.
  • Once you leave the campaign your main priority should be to cap your resistances and increases your Evasion/Energy Shield.
  • A good Hybrid Body Armour is the best defensive purchase for a number of reasons;
    • You need at least 850 Evasion and 300 Energy Shield to reach the Spirit requirements for this build.
    • A large Evasion roll adds a good amount of recovery due to Ghost Dance.
  • Cap your resistances without using glove suffixes if possible as Gloves have some great modifiers for damage in the suffixes.
  • For the weapon, a 100%+ roll of Elemental Damage with Attacks is massive for damage as you don't get a ton of increased damage on this build. After that focus on Critical Hit Chance, Attack Speed and High DPS.
  • The Endgame setup uses Chaos Inoculation to become completely immune to Chaos Damage.
    • You can reach up to 10000 Energy Shield with Endgame Gear on this setup making you extremely Tanky.
    • Meditate adds another 50%, making your effective hit pool; 15000.
  • Dampening Shield and Stun and Ailment Threshold from Energy Shield are essential to prevent Stuns.
  • Rakiata's Flow is a massive damage increase but iis very expensive. Until achieving this, for more consistent damage, replace …and I Shall Rage with Sunder my Enemies….

FAQ

Early Access
Ice Strike Invoker

Yeah...the last few zones of Act 2 are by far the hardest part of this leveling journey for Invoker Monk. If you've followed the previous tips on gearing, you'll be more than capable of clearing this zone. Slowly. Pull packs of enemies and move backward across each of the walkways whilst clearing from a distance. A Stone Charm can do wonders here if you are fortunate enough to find one!

If you get jumped on at any point, move backward. Always try to keep a distance in this zone and take it 1 pack at a time as dying here is incredibly unforgiving.

You can also reset the final checkpoint in the first dreadnaught zone to fight a guaranteed blue pack that spawns. This can be a great way to get additional exp and items.

This is a hybrid attribute build. You need to alternate Intelligence and Dexterity to grab all the skills shown in the planner. Remember you can switch between them by Refunding Passives in town.

During the early acts there are not that many defensive options for Monk. Your best bet is checking vendors consistently for life and resistance gear.

  • Relic: 15% increased Mana Recovery from Flasks
  • Venom Drought: Stun Threshold
  • Relic: 15% increased Life Recovery from Flasks
  • Venom Drought: +5 All Attributes
  • Tattoos: Dex, Intelligence, Fire Resistance

Act 4 and the interlude acts are a massive difficulty jump. Good practices, such as always checking vendors and adding runes to sure up resistances is crucial for success here.

Whakapanu Island is particularly dangerous, make sure you have atleast 30+ Cold Resistance and a Thawing Charm before attempting.
