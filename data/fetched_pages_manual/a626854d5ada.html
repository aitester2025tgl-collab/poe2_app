Boneshatter Titan Build Guide

Early AccessThe Third Edict - 0.3

Last Updated:September 9, 2025|Changelog|FAQ

!
Third Edict Update

0.3.0 gives the Warrior an interesting new way to deal single target damage with Forge Hammer, he now has access to Strike Melee Splash thanks to Tribal Fury and enjoys an overall boost in power compared to 0.2.0. This is due to buffs to Armour, Support Gems and new nodes on the tree.

Become the Titan and hit your enemies with a big stick until their bones shatter. Should they survive that, you could always drop a massive hammer on their heads to make sure they're not getting back up. And the Boneshatter Titan Build Guide will show you how.

Prime enemies for stun by Leap Slamming on their heads before you obliterate the whole pack with Boneshatter. Explode giant rock pillars with Earthshatter and drop the Hammer of the Gods on anything that dares you to bring out the big gun.

This build doesn't require any Unique items to function. But the build does benefit from common Uniques including Thrillsteel.

!
Resistances

You do not need specific resistances on any item, just get the required total resistances amongst all gear combined. You need to cap your Resistances at 75% unless otherwise specified in the guide!

Weapon Swap Mechanics
  • Boneshatter causes a large AoE explosion when used on mobs which are Primed for Stun and is your bread and butter skill to clear packs.
    • Check the Skill Rotation section below for more information on Priming.
  • Prime Enemies for stun with Mace Strike (Leap Slam once you get it) and then use Boneshatter for a huge AoE explosion.
  • Use Earthquake and Mace Strike on anything that survives your Boneshatter.
    • Drop Earthquake on bosses after you stun them.
  • Shockwave Totem further supplements early-game single target damage
    • Use it on Bosses and strong Rares for extra damage
    • Shockwave Totem synergizes with Earthquake's Jagged Ground as it causes it to erupt every time it slams.
    • Place the Totem behind the Boss so it survives for longer as the Boss is mainly trying to hit you.
  • Cast Infernal Cry before using Earthquake for additional damage.
  • Perfect Strike is your main single target damage source during campaign and replaces Earthquake. This is SUPER important to the skills damage output: Hold down your Perfect Strike button until your weapon causes a distinct red flash. Release your button as the flash happens to do massively more damage. Play around with the timing window until it feels comfortable! Utilize your stun windows in order to hit your Perfect Strikes without any danger.
    • The skill has a long windup but deals big damage that inflicts a guaranteed ignite. Use it during situations where you won't get hit by telegraphed attacks or while the boss is stunned
  • Leap Slam is a movement ability that has excellent stun buildup, which makes it the best choice to prime targets for stun while staying mobile. Crater allows you to actually deal very good damage with it by just leaping on enemies!
  • Forge Hammer creates Molten Fissures when exploded by any Warcry. These Fissures erupt when hit by any Slam. Use Shockwave Totem and Leap Slam in order to proc as many as possible!
  • Hammer of the Gods is your heavy hitter. If you encounter a very strong rare, use Infernal Cry to empower it then drop the Hammer!
  • Earthshatter spawns spikes which explode for massive damage when you use Seismic Cry or Infernal Cry. Pre-stack these while a boss is spawning or right before activating an essence. This gives you a big burst of damage and instantly stuns the target.
  • Against bosses, use Earthshatter until the boss is Heavy Stunned, then use Seismic Cry and Infernal Cry before dropping the Hammer.
  • Herald of Ash
    • Activate it by opening your Skill Gem Menu and Right Clicking the skill icon left of the skill name. To see if it's active check if your Spirit shows 0/30 and the Herald of Ash icon swirls.
  • Scavenged Plating grants a sizeable Armour boost and is maintained with Shattering Blow and Splinter.

There are a few things to keep in mind when using this feature:

  • Unsure how to set up your Weapon Swap Sets properly? Click here!
  • ONLY weapon swap to use Hammer of the Gods.
  • Set your warcries to ONLY be used with Weapon Set 1, otherwise, you lose your exerts whenever you weapon swap.
  • Do NOT set your Hammer of the Gods to only Weapon Set 2, as you want to be able to use it freely with your regular Mace+Shield setup while mapping. Swapping away from your shield in the middle of a map gets you killed.
    • Note that this means you always have to manually swap to Weapon Set 2 if you want your hammer to deal the maximum amounts of damage. Thankfully, that is something you should only need against bosses.
  • It is good practice to set all your abilities other than Hammer of the Gods to only be usable on Weapon Set 1, as the Weapon Set 2 is ONLY used for Hammer of the Gods. That way, you never have to manually swap back to your Weapon Set 1.

Single Target
Clear
  1. Use Earthshatter and blow up its spikes with Seismic Cry/Infernal Cry on anything that survived your Boneshatter.
  2. Use Hammer of the Gods after empowering it with Infernal Cry on particularly tanky enemies.
  3. For Bosses: Pre-stack as many Earthshatter spikes as possible on top of the boss and explode them with Seismic Cry when they become targetable. Keep doing this until the boss gets heavy stunned, then use both Seismic Cry and Infernal Cry, weapon swap to the dual maces and drop Hammer of the Gods.
  4. ONLY WEAPON SWAP IF YOU HAVE 2 EXTRA MACES SIMILAR TO YOUR MAIN WEAPON! If you only have 1 or 2 good maces available to you, do not bother weapon swapping.

Small extra optimization:

  • You often start a boss fight with enough Glory to use Hammer of the Gods straight away.
    • Use it, and put down spikes while the hammer is dropping.
  • Wait out any Hammer Aftershocks (as Heavy Stunning with Hammer does not generate Glory), then explode you Earthshatter spikes to Heavy Stun the boss to generate enough Glory for another Hammer of the Gods.

If you want more details about weapon swapping, check out this article.

Prime enemies for stun by Leap Slamming into them and use Boneshatter to explode the pack. While clearing, simply Leap Slam onto monsters and use Boneshatter to explode the primed targets. You can identify a target primed for stun with a glowing orb displayed on top of them. Preferably use Infernal Cry to ensure rare monsters die from the explosions.

Animation Cancelling

Proper Dodge Roll usage improves the feel of the build massively. Once perfected, Leap Slam -> Dodge Roll -> Boneshatter a primed mob -> Dodge Roll becomes a very natural and fluid combo. The goal is to roll out of the "heavy landing" by holding down your Dodge Roll button shortly before you land with Leap Slam.

Offensive Scaling

  • The biggest source of damage comes from your weapons. Getting good Weapons is your primary focus when trying to gear up.
  • You increase your weapons DPS by looking for the following stats, sorted by order of importance:
    • High physical damage on the base weapon type
    • % increased Physical Damage
    • Adds # to # Physical Damage
    • %# increased Attack Speed
    • Adds # to # Fire/Cold/Lightning Damage
  • Having a source of Mana leech is very valuable. You can get it on your Weapon, Gloves or Rings.
  • Lighting damage to Attacks from either Rings or Gloves is pretty valuable as it allows your Hammer of the Gods to inflict a sizable shock on the target.
  • Sources of #% increased damage: #% Melee Damage, #% Global Physical Damage, #% increased Damage, #% Increased Damage with Maces, #% Area Damage, #% Damage with Two Handed Weapons, #% Attack Damage.

Defensive Scaling

  • Prioritize +#% to Fire, Cold and Lightning Resistance until the 75% cap
  • +# to Maximum Life.
  • +# to Strength because it gives you more maximum life.
  • A Solid Shield. Block is your biggest defensive layer while mapping so try to max it out as soon as possible. The Surrender is a good option for Mapping survivability. When block capped through your passive tree and jewels, it makes you nearly unkillable when fighting large amounts of monsters.
  • If you are still struggling check out our Survival Guide for additional useful information!

On top of the basic requirements of the Campaign tab you should:

  • Prioritize maximum Armour on:
    • Helmet
    • Body Armour
    • Gloves
    • Boots (Make sure they have high movement speed though!)
  • Acquire a Shield to use together with your 2-handed Mace after allocating Giant's Blood
  • Work towards your weapon swap setup by getting 2 extra maces, being able to swap to a Dual Mace Weapon Swap to drop Hammer of the Gods is a massive boss damage increase.
  • Gloves and Weapons with Attack Speed are also a big boost as it makes the build feel much smoother to play and increases your clear speed significantly.
  • Keep improving your gear with better Life and Resistance rolls and acquire the relatively common Unique the build utilizes.

Pick Strength for every travel node, pathing to the Polymathy cluster allows to get a good chunk of extra strength, and more importantly, the small Reduced Attribute Requirements passives significantly reduce the amount of strength needed to meet the requirements. Other than that, just try to look for as much Strength as possible from gear and do not feel bad about using a lower tier weapon. A well rolled mid tier weapon is good enough to last you until you have enough Strength to use the highest tier weapons.

The red and green lines represent the weapon set specific passive points.
When you are using your Weapon set 1 (which is your 2H + Shield setup), the tree allocated includes the red passives. These are passives you have to allocate by first clicking the "Weapon Set 1" arrow at the top right of your passive skill tree.

When you are swapping to your Weapon set 2 (which is Dual Wield 2-handed Maces), the tree allocated should include the green passives. Again, these passives have to be allocated after clicking the 2nd arrow at the top right to switch to "Allocating points for Weapon Set 2."

The passives that aren't colored are simply allocated normally without doing anything in particular.

  • Make sure your Leap Slam gem stays at low level to prevent it from killing monsters.
  • Make sure it is linked to the Brink support as this is what enables you to NOT heavy stun monsters instead of simply priming them for stun, which enables Boneshatter to be used.
  • Make sure it is linked to the Overpower support as it allows even a low level Leap Slam to still build up enough stun to prime targets.

It is very important to make sure you follow the single target damage rotation. Not using your Warcries properly can result in your Hammer of the Gods dealing a small fraction of the damage it is supposed to.

Note

Infernal Cry provides a huge amount of damage to Earthshatter as well, so if your Hammer of the Gods is not ready yet or you do not feel like you need to use it yet, do not hesitate to use Infernal Cry to boots your Earthshatter damage as it has a fairly low cooldown.

  • Do NOT level your Leap Slam, Infernal Cry or Seismic Cry gems. It is still useful to level you Infernal Cry gem for extra Warcry Speed, but only do it if your mana feels comfortable OR you have a 5th slot to support it with Lifetap.

Summary

Early Access - The Third Edict
Boneshatter Titan Build Guide

The Boneshatter Titan Build Guide teaches you the following things in-depth:

  • Leap Slam and Boneshatter work together to enable massive screen wide explosions.
  • Boneshatter mechanics allow for absurdly high damage in high density. Fighting an extremely tough Rare or Essence? Fight it in a giant pack of monsters and watch it get obliterated.
  • Earthshatter makes quick work of surviving rares and has very good pre-stacking damage and stun potential to start off boss fights with a very early Heavy Stun.
  • Hammer of the Gods takes advantage of heavy stuns to deal massive amounts of damage by utilizing the weapon swapping mechanic.
  • Block allows you to mitigate 50% of the attacks coming your way, with The Surrender making you even more resilient if you get overwhelmed.
  • BIG BOOM.
