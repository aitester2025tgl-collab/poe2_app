Build Overview

This is a detailed LEVELING guide for INSTANT Earthquake Warrior later Titan (see my other guides)

Strengths and Weaknesses

  • Damage both ST and AoE
  • Speed (Leap or Shield Charge)
  • Massive AoE
  • Can be a bit squishy during the campaign (depends on gear you find as armor plays a huge role)

Gem Priority

You will get Rolling Slam in the tutorial from a chest and once you arrive to Clearfell go with Earthquake from the first skill gem as a quest reward.

First support gem pick Martial Tempo in Rolling Slam (RS) and then Impact Shockwave in Boneshatter as that is our MAIN combo - you use Rolling Slam and right after the first hit you animation cancel the second hit by pressing Boneshatter (BS). In most cases RS primes enemies for a heavy stun which we trigger with BS.

After that it's time for Fast Forward and Swift Affliction for Earthquake because once you get strong enough weapon you can literally EQ and roll dodge as you move while detonations aka Aftershocks from EQ kill mobs behind you.

Rage can be slotted in Mace Strike if you want to get some generation going but I haven't really found any use. MAYBE with Cultist Greathammer in Act 2 as it's a decent clear.

First Lesser jeweler's orb put into Earthquake and add a damage gem maybe Lightning Infusion or Fire Infusion depends on which flat damage you have on gear. Later on once you find level 2 support gems put Close Combat instead.

First Ascendancy is pretty much free whatever you like more also depends on current setup. Extra life is good for sustain if you die a lot or even armor increase as it's quite good in campaign. Crushing blows are also decent for easy stuns mainly bosses where Rolling Slam comes in as the second slam has multiplicative damage into stunned enemies. Second Ascendancy is in next tab in Build variants.

For mobility Shield Charge is an option on Weapon set 2 as weapon swapping is instant now.

FIRST Spirit gem from Freythorn Act 1 get War Banner and SECOND Spirit gem from Azak Bog Act 3 pick Time of Need which also gets faster with our reduced skill effect duration stacking OR Herald of Ash for more AoE.

How it Plays

1. Rolling Slam - For AoE you let RS hit once and right after it hits you use 2. to cancel it's second hit and explode monsters. For single target you keep rollin' and slammin'

2. Boneshatter - as mentioned above this is our AoE clear early on

3. Earthquake - once you get good enough weapon or flat damage rings all you do is EQ and roll dodge. This will be constantly moving while it's aftershocks kill mobs behind you. Very smooth and fast.

4. Infernal Cry - always save for heavy stun phase with 1. for juicy damage. EASY bossing

5. Shield Charge / Leap Slam - our mobility skills early on its Charge with Weapon set 2 as we use 2H Mace for damage on Weapon set 1 until you level up to get Leap which you can use freely. Later on with Giant's Blood and shield in off hand Shield Charge is BiS

6. Seismic Cry - use during a heavy stun which will copy your slams in this case 1. and watch the health bar on bosses MELT

7. Hammer of the Gods - this can be our opener on bosses for stunning them or during a heavy stun with 6. also easy to use on rares as a range ability if there are too many mobs around.

How it Works

Priming enemies for a heavy stun from Rolling Slam's first hit into Boneshatter for AoE. With good enough weapon/gear Earthquake and roll is the move for smooth sailing. Single target you just Rolling Slam.

Seismic Cry later on in combination with Aftershocks and Rolling Slam or Hammer of the Gods from support gems and Ascendancy/Passive tree is some crazy single target damage.
