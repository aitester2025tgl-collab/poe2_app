Build Overview

This is my leveling setup, can be done on both Ranger or Huntress if you want an easy and SSF viable way to do campaign in 0.3 - Below is a link to my endgame setup that is also updated for 0.3

  • Fubgun Lightning Arrow Endgame Setup

The transition requires very little gold, but be sure not to gamble away all of it so you can respec your tree to the new build.

Early boss kill order for a smooth experience:
Devourer > cold witch > hut witch > rust king
Grab lightning infusion after killing devourer.

In act 1 it's very important to have gear with flat damage to attack, this can be any element or phys. At lvl 11 you want to get a Warden Bow with 1-2 sockets, place a desert, glacier or storm rune in here for a big boost in DPS (storm rune is the best, but any will do).

Also look at the vendor in town every 1-2 lvls for flat damage gloves, every flat for the build is massive in at 1

Movement speed on boots is also extremely good if you can find them, try to search the vendor every 1-2 lvls. I would never swap out movement speed boots, only replace them with other boots that have movement speed.

Besides that you're just looking for life/resist on gear, but flat damage is the most important on gloves/rings.

!!!!Important!!!!!
Always salvage weapons with quality for whetstones and always salvage items with sockets for artificers orb, this is extremely important for gear upgrades throughout the campaign.

I also personally prefer to vendor all rares for gold to gamble in act 2 and act 3 for better gear.

Gem Priority

Contagion is really good for lvls 1-4, after the redvale zone you should drop it.

Ideally auto attack with crossbow is your best dps option until lvl 6 with Stormcaller Arrow, but you're fine to just use la+rod as well.

LA+Rod is your ideal setup for Rust king in redvale, can be hard to use on the witches and I wouldn't use it on devourer (bow shot or crossbow auto attack is better)

In general just use dexterity for your small attribute nodes. Use stregnth/int if you need it for gear or support gems.

Deadeye Ascendancy points:

In order: Gathering winds > Point blank > Endless munitions.

How it Plays

Lightning Arrow and Freezing Salvo to clear packs. Depending on your damage, you'll want to use Lightning Rod once into a Barrage > Lightning Arrow for rares.

On bosses use Freezing Salvo on cooldown and place down multiple Lightning Rod(usually aim for 8-10 on bosses, can pre-fire them on most bosses).
After that you're spamming Lightning Arrow and using Barrage on cooldown.
