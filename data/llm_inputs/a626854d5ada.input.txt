Boneshatter Titan Build Guide Gear FAQ Early Access - The Third Edict Boneshatter Titan Build Guide How do I get enough Strength for Giant's Blood ? Pick Strength for every travel node, pathing to the Polymathy cluster allows to get a good chunk of extra strength, and more importantly, the small Reduced Attribute Requirements passives significantly reduce the amount of strength needed to meet the requirements. Other than that, just try to look for as much Strength as possible from gear and do not feel bad about using a lower tier weapon. A well rolled mid tier weapon is good enough to last you until you have enough Strength to use the highest tier weapons. What do the red and green lines mean on the Passive Tree? The red and green lines represent the weapon set specific passive points. When you are using your Weapon set 1 (which is your 2H + Shield setup), the tree allocated includes the red passives. These are passives you have to allocate by first clicking the "Weapon Set 1" arrow at the top right of your passive skill tree. When you are swapping to your Weapon set 2 (which is Dual Wield 2-handed Maces), the tree allocated should include the green passives. Again, these passives have to be allocated after clicking the 2nd arrow at the top right to switch to "Allocating points for Weapon Set 2." The passives that aren't colored are simply allocated normally without doing anything in particular. My Leap Slam does not prime monsters and I cannot use Boneshatter. Make sure your Leap Slam gem stays at low level to prevent it from killing monsters. Make sure it is linked to the Brink support as this is what enables you to NOT heavy stun monsters instead of simply priming them for stun, which enables Boneshatter to be used. Make sure it is linked to the Overpower support as it allows even a low level Leap Slam to still build up enough stun to prime targets. I struggle with single target on bosses, how to fix it ? It is very important to make sure you follow the single target damage rotation. Not using your Warcries properly can result in your Hammer of the Gods dealing a small fraction of the damage it is supposed to. Note Infernal Cry provides a huge amount of damage to Earthshatter as well, so if your Hammer of the Gods is not ready yet or you do not feel like you need to use it yet, do not hesitate to use Infernal Cry to boots your Earthshatter damage as it has a fairly low cooldown. How do I fix mana problems? A single Mana Leech roll on a Ring or Gloves solves all your mana issues. Do NOT level your Leap Slam , Infernal Cry or Seismic Cry gems. It is still useful to level you Infernal Cry gem for extra Warcry Speed, but only do it if your mana feels comfortable OR you have a 5th slot to support it with Lifetap .
