Vendor Regex: "y: \+|ts: S|\d+% i.+mov|to atta|ll pro|ward|dual" (You copy and paste this into your vendor window, to easily see items you might want to buy) Build Variants lvl 1-5 lvl 6-14 lvl 15-27 [Advance] 1-27 lvl 28-40 lvl 40-60+ Twink uniques Use the build variants above to see skill tree progression and gear upgrades. Equipment lvl 1-5 lvl 6-14 lvl 15-27 [Advance] 1-27 lvl 28-40 lvl 40-60+ Twink uniques Set 1 Set 2 Early boss kill order for a smooth experience: Devourer > cold witch > hut witch > rust king Grab lightning infusion after killing devourer. In act 1 it's very important to have gear with flat damage to attack, this can be any element or phys. At lvl 11 you want to get a Warden Bow with 1-2 sockets, place a desert, glacier or storm rune in here for a big boost in DPS (storm rune is the best, but any will do). Also look at the vendor in town every 1-2 lvls for flat damage gloves, every flat for the build is massive in at 1 Movement speed on boots is also extremely good if you can find them, try to search the vendor every 1-2 lvls. I would never swap out movement speed boots, only replace them with other boots that have movement speed. Besides that you're just looking for life/resist on gear, but flat damage is the most important on gloves/rings. !!!!Important!!!!! Always salvage weapons with quality for whetstones and always salvage items with sockets for artificers orb, this is extremely important for gear upgrades throughout the campaign. I also personally prefer to vendor all rares for gold to gamble in act 2 and act 3 for better gear. Skill Gems lvl 1-5 lvl 6-14 lvl 15-27 [Advance] 1-27 lvl 28-40 lvl 40-60+ Twink uniques Support Gem Requirements: Str 0 Dex 10 Int 0 Expand all 1 Bow Shot 2 Lightning Arrow 3 Lightning Rod 4 Contagion Gem Priority Track This Build Contagion is really good for lvls 1-4, after the redvale zone you should drop it. Ideally auto attack with crossbow is your best dps option until lvl 6 with Stormcaller Arrow , but you're fine to just use la+rod as well. LA+Rod is your ideal setup for Rust king in redvale, can be hard to use on the witches and I wouldn't use it on devourer (bow shot or crossbow auto attack is better) Passive Tree lvl 1-5 lvl 6-14 lvl 15-27 [Advance] 1-27 lvl 28-40 lvl 40-60+ Twink uniques main : 123 123 set 1 : 20 20 set 2 : 20 20 In general just use dexterity for your small attribute nodes. Use stregnth/int if you need it for gear or support gems. Deadeye Ascendancy points: In order: Gathering winds > Point blank > Endless munitions. How it Plays Lightning Arrow and Freezing Salvo to clear packs. Depending on your damage, you'll want to use Lightning Rod once into a Barrage > Lightning Arrow for rares. On bosses use Freezing Salvo on cooldown and place down multiple Lightning Rod (usually aim for 8-10 on bosses, can pre-fire them on most bosses). After that you're spamming Lightning Arrow and using Barrage on cooldown. How it Works Quest rewards to take: 1) charm recharge - act 2 2) stun threshold from venom reward - act 3 3) tattoos - lightning resist/int/fire resist - act 4 4) boon - movement speed - interlude Deadeye Ascendancy points: In order: Gathering winds > Point blank > Endless munitions.
