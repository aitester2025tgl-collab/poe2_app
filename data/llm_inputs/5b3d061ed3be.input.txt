First support gem pick Martial Tempo in Rolling Slam (RS) and then Impact Shockwave in Boneshatter as that is our MAIN combo - you use Rolling Slam and right after the first hit you animation cancel the second hit by pressing Boneshatter (BS). In most cases RS primes enemies for a heavy stun which we trigger with BS. After that it's time for Fast Forward and Swift Affliction for Earthquake because once you get strong enough weapon you can literally EQ and roll dodge as you move while detonations aka Aftershocks from EQ kill mobs behind you. Rage can be slotted in Mace Strike if you want to get some generation going but I haven't really found any use. MAYBE with Cultist Greathammer in Act 2 as it's a decent clear. First Lesser jeweler's orb put into Earthquake and add a damage gem maybe Lightning Infusion or Fire Infusion depends on which flat damage you have on gear. Later on once you find level 2 support gems put Close Combat instead. First Ascendancy is pretty much free whatever you like more also depends on current setup. Extra life is good for sustain if you die a lot or even armor increase as it's quite good in campaign. Crushing blows are also decent for easy stuns mainly bosses where Rolling Slam comes in as the second slam has multiplicative damage into stunned enemies. Second Ascendancy is in next tab in Build variants. For mobility Shield Charge is an option on Weapon set 2 as weapon swapping is instant now. FIRST Spirit gem from Freythorn Act 1 get War Banner and SECOND Spirit gem from Azak Bog Act 3 pick Time of Need which also gets faster with our reduced skill effect duration stacking OR Herald of Ash for more AoE.
