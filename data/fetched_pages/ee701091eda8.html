Minion Army Infernalist Leveling Guide

Early AccessThe Third Edict - 0.3

Last Updated:September 22, 2025|Changelog|FAQ

This Minion Army Infernalist Leveling Guide for the Witch Class gives you a detailed step-by-step explanation of how to properly build your character. Follow along with the same build I used in Early Access to make your campaign playthrough a lot more enjoyable, the first few levels can be a bit rough until you get the Raging Spirits skill.

If you're into commanding an army of minions this is the build for you!



Note: If you find Exalted Orbs or Chaos Orbs during the Campaign use them to buy Enfolding Dawn and Bones of Ullr from other players. Later at level 51 Waveshaper is a great defensive option instead of Enfolding Dawn.

!
Resistances and Attributes

You do not need specific resistances and attributes on any item, just get the required total resistances and attributes amongst all gear combined. You need to cap your Resistances at 75% unless otherwise specified in the guide!

Early Access

Path of Exile 2 is currently in early access, this means rapid development including frequent changes to builds as there are balance fixes and we learn more. Pin this page to be notified of any updates.

Endgame guide

If you've beaten the campaign and want to check out an endgame version of the build check out our guide here!
If you prefer a build with a lot less visual clutter and if you suffer from performance issues you can follow our Skeletal Sniper guide here or the cold Spectre guide here!

Skills

Early Access - The Third Edict
Minion Army Infernalist Leveling

During the campaign, you summon Raging Spirits by casting Flame Wall. Then, use the Minion command skill provided by Skeletal Sniper to deal area wide damage. You also cast Elemental Weakness to increase your Minion's damage. Unearth is cast on enemy corpses to spawn an army of temporary Minions. You also buff your army of Minions with Pain Offering to increase their damage.

Note: Do not use Spirit on more Skeletal Warriors from your Rattling Sceptre.

Act 1

  • The first gem you find is Unearth it is used to deal AoE damage and to spawn more Minions from dead enemy corpses.
  • With your first Uncut Skill Gem cut Skeletal Sniper, it is used to deal damage to your enemies and to gain access to a minion command skill that allows you to send a Gas Arrow. (If you have enough Spirit you can enable the minion by clicking on the "+" sign in the skills menu)
    Cut Multishot I as your first support gem for this skill.
  • The second Skill Gem you cut is Flame Wall it is used to buff ranged projectiles with fire damage and to deal more AoE damage to enemies. You find this skillgem by clicking the tab "Elemental". Flame Wall ignites enemies which causes the Gas Arrow command from Skeletal Snipers to explode for very high damage. Make sure you always use this against rares and bosses. Make sure to keep Flame Wall at level 7 to reduce the mana cost.
    Cut Fire Attunement for your Skeletal Snipers so they deal even more damage with the command skill and their auto attack.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!
  • The third Skill Gem you cut is Contagion. It is used to deal AoE Chaos damage that can spread to your Unearth Minions.
    Cut Unleash as your first support gem for this skill.
  • The fourth Skill Gem you cut is Frost Bomb. It is used to deal AoE damage and apply elemental exposure which reduces enemies elemental resistances. By reducing enemies fire resistance the damage of your Skeletal Snipers command skill is increased.
  • Using a level 3 Uncut Skill Gem you get Essence Drain for improved single target damage.

Note: When you level up your Skeletal Sniper skill to level 3 you can summon one more since the Spirit cost has been reduced.

  • Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!
  • After defeating The King in the Mists around level 10-13, you get an Uncut Spirit Gem (Level 4). With this you must get Raging Spirits (you need to change from the Witch skill tab to the Sorceress tab to find it).
    Raging Spirits are summoned by casting Flame Wall which you need to cut if you have not done so yet.

    Try to keep 10 raging spirits up at all times during a boss fight.
    Move Last Gasp and Fire Attunement into your Raging Spirits for increased damage.
    Cut a new Last Gasp for your Skeletal Snipers.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!

Note: You need to activate the Raging Spirits buff for them to be summoned when you cast Flame Wall. To do this, go to the Skills Panel and right click the Raging Spirits icon.

  • Using a level 5 Uncut Skill Gem cut Pain Offering to boost the damage of all your Minions by offering one of your skeletons on a bone spike.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!

Act 2

  • Using a level 7 Uncut Skill Gem get Elemental Weakness, this curse increases your minions damage against the cursed targets.
  • If you have Enfolding Dawn, you now have the option to swap out your Skeletal Snipers with level 7 Skeletal Arsonists. If you do not yet have this unique then wait at least until you are able to summon 4-6 Skeletal Arsonists. The Skeletal Sniper command skill is just too powerful to give up if you do not have multiple extra Skeletal Arsonists.

You can find a higher level Uncut Spirit Gem from the Golden Tomb side quest in The Lost City area. Use it to level up your Raging Spirits so that they deal even more damage.

  • Finish Trial of the Sekhemas when you feel confident enough to gain access to your Loyal Hellhound.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!

Act 3

  • Cut Loyalty for your Summon Infernal Hound.
  • The first Lesser Jeweller's Orb you find is used on Skeletal Sniper or Skeletal Arsonist. Cut Feeding Frenzy and place in the third slot.
  • The second Lesser Jeweller's Orb you find is used on Raging Spirits, Cut Feeding Frenzy to place in the new third support gem slot.
  • You use no new gems at level 9 Uncut Skill Gem.
    Upgrade your main damage minion first.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!
  • You use no new gems at level 11 Uncut Skill Gem.
    Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display!

Note: When you reach the end of Act 3 in the "Utzaal" area you have the option to replace your Skeletal Arsonist with Spectres. Use the Bind Spectre gem to get Vaal Guard (Bombers). Make sure you bind the one that casts bombs, there are three different type of monsters with the exact same name "Vaal Guard". One of them is melee (50 Spirit cost), one is ranged (60 Spirit cost), one casts bombs (50 Spirit cost). Use the same supports for Bind Spectre as you do for Skeletal Arsonist.
Be warned this Spectre can cause performance issues but it is very powerful, if your computer is unable to handle these Spectres keep using Skeletal Arsonist instead.

Act 4 & Interludes

  • Using a level 13 Uncut Skill Gem cut Skeletal Cleric, they heal all your other Minions and revive your other Skeletons. You only need one or two of this support Minion.
    Cut Last Gasp as the first support gem for this skill.
  • Make sure to update your support gems as they are in the skills display with the rest of the level 3 supports!
  • You need to get one Skeletal Storm Mage to debuff enemies with shock and improve the damage bonus from Muster.

For the attribute nodes, take enough Strength and Dexterity to meet gear and support gem requirements, then use the rest on Intelligence. You can respec your attributes for gold if the requirements on your gear suddenly changes instead of waiting to level up.

Ascendancy

Early Access - The Third Edict
Minion Army Infernalist Leveling
How to Ascend

Learn how to complete the Trial of the Sekhemas and Trial of Chaos to earn your first 2 Ascensions with our Ascension Guide!

  • 1st Ascension : Loyal Hellhound.
    • To ignite enemies and apply fire exposure when supported by Fire Exposure and for 10% damage reduction when supported by Loyalty.
  • 2nd Ascension: Altered Flesh.
    • This helps deal with Physical damage if you have Chaos Resistance. When you start stacking Maximum Fire resistances, you take less Cold and Lightning damage.
  • 3rd Ascension: Beidat's Will
    • For more maximum Spirit at the cost of 25% life reservation.
  • 4th Ascension: Beidat's Hand or two Life.
    • The default choice is Beidat's Hand.
    • However, if you have less than 1400 life and Low Chaos resistance, take two Life nodes instead.

Gearing

Early Access - The Third Edict
Minion Army Infernalist Leveling
  • By far the best way to get passable gear in the campaign is to check the Vendor in Town each time you level up! As vendors change their offerings based on your level this is an excellent way to find extra upgrades.
  • Most of the damage you take during the campaign outside of Boss/Rare encounters is Physical Damage. Armour reduces the damage you take from Physical Damage, so look out for hybrid Armour & Energy Shield or pure Energy Shield rolls on your gear. Chaos resistance helps to deal with Physical mitigation when you have your second Ascendancy Altered Flesh.
  • Prioritize +#% to Fire, Cold and Lightning Resistance as well as +# to Maximum Life on your gear.
  • A great source is checking vendors occasionally for promising bases to craft on with Orbs of Transmutation, Orbs of Augmentation, Regal Orbs, Exalted Orbs, Chaos Orbs and Essences.
  • Use a Shield over a wand or a Focus!
    A Shield provides passive block chance against attack and spell damage which is a very strong defensive layer.
  • Looks for Sceptres with the following modifiers:
    Grants skill: Skeletal Warrior.
    • 1-4 Level of all Minion Skills (The by far best modifier)
    • % Increased Spirit
    • Allies in your presence deal #% Increased Damage
    • Allies in your presence have #% Increased Attack Speed
    • Allies in your presence deal # to # Additional Attack Lightning/Fire/Cold/Physical Damage
  • If you find a very good Sceptre with multiple of the above mods, but without the Skeletal Warrior skill you should still use it.
  • Use resistance runes on your equipment!
  • Use a Primate Talisman or a Serpent Talisman for your Sceptre.
  • Make sure to check out where you can find all permanent character stat bonuses here!

FAQ

Early Access - The Third Edict
Minion Army Infernalist Leveling

Make sure to use a good Sceptre with correct stats to support your Minions damage!

Get more Spirit on your Body Armour and Amulet to summon more Skeletal Arsonists.
Use an amulet and helmet with +1 Minion Skill levels.

Low priority support gems are:

  • Magnified Effect
  • Spell Cascade
  • Ingenuity
  • Persistence
  • All support gems on your Skeletal Warrior

Until you get Essence Drain or Raging Spirits the build does suffer from a lack of single target damage.

Act 2: 30% Increased Charm Charges gained & +1 Charm Slot.
Act 3: 25% Increased Mana Regeneration Rate
Act 4: 30% Increased Life Recovery from Flasks and Strength, Int and Dex tattoos.
Interlude: There are multiple good options here depending on what you need such as Attributes, Resistances, Global Defenses and Movement Speed. (This choice can be changed later).

Note: These bonuses are minor. If you picked something different, don't worry too much!

If you have Skeletal Warrior from a Sceptre, then the amount of gem sockets depends on the skill level from the Sceptre you are using.

The gem sockets for your Loyal Hellhound depend on your own level, at level 90 you get the last socket slot for it.
